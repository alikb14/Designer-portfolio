# Yaad Motion Portfolio — Definition of Done

A change is done only when all applicable conditions are satisfied or explicitly waived.

- Acceptance criteria are met and traced to observed or automated evidence.
- Reference fidelity is reviewed at 320, 375, 768, 1024, 1440, and 1920 px where applicable.
- Public behavior and its CMS edit/publish path are completed together for content-driven slices.
- Hover behavior has keyboard/touch equivalents; reduced-motion and no-canvas fallbacks are covered.
- Videos are poster-backed, lazy/visibility-aware, correctly delivered, and do not all preload.
- Earth frame time, cleanup, visibility pause, stable visual density, and context-loss recovery meet approved targets.
- Drafts/unpublished content cannot leak; every mutation is provider/server authorized.
- Uploads, URLs, slugs, rich text, webhooks, downloads, and optional Contact input are validated.
- Loading, empty, error, permission, processing, degraded, recovery, and not-found states are complete.
- Formatter, lint, type, unit, integration, E2E, accessibility, security, performance, and production build checks pass where applicable.
- Both themes, supported browsers, keyboard, touch, 200% zoom, and reduced motion are manually reviewed.
- Configuration, deployment, migration, backup/restore, rollback, monitoring, and runbooks are documented and tested in proportion to risk.
- No secrets or private media are committed or logged.
- CMS field help and owner guide are complete; Yaad can publish without developer guidance.
- Documentation, ADRs, ledger, and handoff match actual approved/implemented/verified state.
- Verified, unverified, assumed, blocked, accepted-risk, and deferred items are reported separately.
